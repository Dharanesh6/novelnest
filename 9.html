Man's Search for Meaning

Explore the profound journey of resilience, purpose, and hope in this timeless classic.

Author: Viktor E. Frankl

Genre: Psychology / Philosophy

Published: 1946

Book Description

Man's Search for Meaning details Viktor Frankl's experiences in Nazi concentration camps and explores his psychotherapeutic method of finding meaning in all forms of existence, even the most brutal ones.
